A little sound.
A shared rhythm.

Loop Together turns everyday sounds into a rhythm you can make with someone else. Record a sound each, choose its beats and listen to your ideas play together.

Enter the sound studio ↗

DESIGN SCENARIO

Collaboration & Social Creation

My project responds to this scenario through shared music-making on one device. It is designed for people who want to experiment together without needing to read music.

MY RESPONSE

Two contributions. One loop.

Each person has a separate sample track. Both tracks use the same eight-beat timeline, so partners can hear how their choices interact. Clear controls, demo sounds and a downloadable mix support exploration and sharing.

From exploration to instrument

This version brings together the step sequencing explored in Prototype 1C, text feedback from 2B and sound-level feedback from 2A. Its compact studio layout puts recording and beat selection close together.
